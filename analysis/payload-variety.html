Key takeaways
The variety is the point. These attackers used at least 6 distinct delivery mechanisms (stdin, wget, curl, scp, base64, /dev/tcp) and 3 execution paths (direct run, cron, systemd) — but every single one had to land code and run it. That's the chokepoint.

Most payloads never touched a download utility. 82.9% of staged-then-executed sessions delivered payloads over the SSH channel's own stdin. A detection rule watching only for wget/curl/tftp catches less than 1 in 5.

Recon was common but not required. 1,344 sessions did recon and stopped. 130 sessions fetched payloads with zero recon. 26 of 27 Mirai-style loaders skipped discovery entirely.

Some operators split the attack across connections. src-5063 pushed a payload in one SSH session and ran it 3 minutes later in a new one — same source, different session IDs.
